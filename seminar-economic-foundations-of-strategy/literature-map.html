How to read this map

The graph groups the readings by the summer's four themes plus Week 2 TCE. Edges mark "conversation / extension / complementarity" relations (direction = who points at whom). Blue boxes are in-course readings (click to open the deep dive); gray boxes are the key external works they cite or converse with (click for the DOI or a Google Scholar query; for books without DOIs the link is a Scholar query). External nodes are drawn from the works actually mentioned in the deep-dive pages, not an exhaustive bibliography.

The TCE cluster connects to the rest of the map through three bridge points: Simon 1947 (bounded rationality, shared with Porter 1991's conversation partners and Csaszar), Coase/Williamson (cited by Durand et al. and Leiblein et al. as the economic-foundations thread), and Barney/Penrose (the competence side of Williamson 1999's governance-vs-competence complementarity). For a one-page digest of every TCE paper mentioned here, see the TCE literature review.
