Authors’ argument / tested mechanism. Hayek argues that the information needed for economic adjustment is never fully available to one mind. The coordination problem therefore includes mobilizing knowledge held by separate individuals, especially knowledge specific to circumstances.

Authors’ argument / tested mechanism. Alchian treats the economic environment as selecting among actions according to realized positive profits. Motivated adaptation, imitation, and trial-and-error generate actions under incomplete information.

Evidence basis. The paper develops a theoretical argument, beginning with an extreme random-behavior model and then adding motivation. The selected introduction and conclusion report this design; they do not provide an original statistical test.

Question. How should activities be distributed among firms, cooperative relationships and market transactions when productive capabilities differ?

Authors’ argument / tested mechanism. Richardson distinguishes similar activities, requiring comparable capabilities, from complementary activities, requiring quantitative or qualitative coordination. Evidence basis. The theoretical argument draws on industrial examples, including Marks and Spencer’s supplier coordination and technology licensing. Examples establish the relevance of intermediate arrangements but are not a systematically sampled empirical test.

Question. Do increases in coordination requirements cause firms to bring professional services inside, and does prior plural sourcing facilitate adjustment?

Evidence basis. The sample includes 217,480 patent applications from 101 regularly patenting Fortune 500 firms during 1995–2005. Differential exposure to the AIPA changes is measured using pre-reform patent-class grant delays.

Claim or finding. Greater exposure to increased coordination requirements was associated with more insourcing, especially among firms that had previously both made and bought prosecution services.

Limits of the evidence. The quasi-experimental interpretation depends on the reform exposure identifying coordination requirements rather than other differential changes. The study does not isolate authority, common language and colocation separately.
