Optional background · Aiden

Eisenhardt (1989)
Agency Theory: An Assessment and Review

Academy of Management Review 14(1): 57–74

Question · What does an agency perspective add to organizational research?

Author’s opening argument · The review organizes agency research around information, incentives, risk, and contractual choice. It distinguishes the positivist and principal–agent streams and develops propositions for organizational research.

Read for · Start here if you need the conceptual map. Track the distinction between behavior-based and outcome-based contracts, then ask how observability and outcome uncertainty change the trade-off.

Discussion prompt (reader-generated) · When would stronger outcome-based pay shift too much risk to the agent?

Abstract and introduction, pp. 57–59; source PDF available. Original publication

Open full bilingual deep dive →

Core · Everyone reads carefully · Aiden presents

Jensen & Meckling (1976)
Theory of the Firm: Managerial Behavior, Agency Costs and Ownership Structure

Journal of Financial Economics 3: 305–360

Question · What happens when decision making is delegated to actors with imperfectly aligned interests?

Author’s opening argument · The paper integrates agency, property-rights, and finance arguments to explain ownership structure. It examines agency costs associated with outside equity and debt, who bears those costs, and why costly agency relationships can persist.

Read for · Reconstruct the owner-manager example. Separate the definition of agency costs from the argument about how investors price anticipated managerial behavior. Keep equity and debt mechanisms distinct.

Discussion prompt (reader-generated) · Why can an ownership arrangement with positive agency costs still be selected by contracting parties?

Abstract and introductory research questions, pp. 305–308; source PDF available. Original publication

Open full bilingual deep dive →

Core · Chunyang presents

Aghion & Tirole (1997)
Formal and Real Authority in Organizations

Journal of Political Economy 105(1): 1–29

Question · Who has the right to decide, and who effectively determines the decision?

Author’s opening argument · Formal authority is the right to decide; real authority is effective control over decisions. Information acquisition connects the two. Delegation can encourage the agent’s initiative while reducing the principal’s control.

Read for · Track both parties’ information-acquisition incentives. Ask why retaining a formal veto need not imply effective control, and how overload, urgency, and preference congruence enter the argument.

Discussion prompt (reader-generated) · How would cheaper information acquisition change initiative as well as control?

Abstract and introduction, pp. 1–4; source PDF available. Original publication

Open full bilingual deep dive →

Core · Yupeng presents · 10 minutes max

Holmström (1999)
The Firm as a Subeconomy

Journal of Law, Economics, & Organization 15(1): 74–102

Question · Why do firms own assets, and how does ownership support an internal incentive system?

Author’s opening argument · Holmström uses asset ownership to connect firm boundaries with internal organization. Control over assets conveys contracting rights, allowing the firm to shape tasks, access, and incentives in a local economic system. The introduction presents this as a partial synthesis and conjecture.

Read for · Follow the progression from Alchian–Demsetz to the critique of Hart–Moore, then to contractual externalities and multitask incentives. Identify why regulating access to assets matters for designing cooperation.

Discussion prompt (reader-generated) · When might limiting individual outside opportunities help sustain internal cooperation, and what constrains this power?

Abstract and introduction, pp. 74–77; source PDF available. Original publication

Open full bilingual deep dive →

Core · Yupeng presents · 10 minutes max

Gartenberg & Zenger (2023)
The Firm as a Subsociety: Purpose, Justice, and the Theory of the Firm

Organization Science 34(5): 1965–1980

Question · What does authority need to sustain when employees become members of a firm?

Author’s opening argument · The authors extend the subeconomy view with membership, common purpose, and justice. Employees exchange rights for membership benefits and develop expectations about how authority will exercise control. Boundary decisions therefore have social as well as economic consequences.

Read for · Read directly after Holmström. Track the rights exchanged on entry, the expectations attached to membership, and the implications for authority and boundary changes.

Discussion prompt (reader-generated) · What observable consequence would distinguish a membership-and-justice explanation from an incentive-system explanation?

Abstract and opening argument, p. 1965; source PDF available. Print year 2023; online publication 2022. Original publication

Open full bilingual deep dive →

Core · Everyone reads carefully · Chunyang presents · Read last

Gibbons (2005)
Four Formal(izable) Theories of the Firm?

Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization 58: 200–245

Question · Are rent-seeking, property-rights, incentive-system, and adaptation theories explaining the same mechanism?

Author’s opening argument · Gibbons compares stripped-down versions of four theories, first verbally, then formally, and finally within an integrative framework. He explicitly limits the scope to selected boundary theories and distinguishes these elemental models from the richer original contributions.

Read for · For each theory, record its definition of integration, the source of its benefit, and the countervailing cost. Use this comparison to revisit the TCE and PRT readings.

Discussion prompt (reader-generated) · What would an empirical study have to measure to discriminate among the four mechanisms?

Abstract and introduction, pp. 200–203; source PDF available. Original publication

选读背景 · Aiden 报告

Eisenhardt (1989)
Agency Theory: An Assessment and Review

Academy of Management Review 14(1): 57–74

核心问题 · 代理理论为组织研究增加了哪些解释？

作者的开篇论点 · 这篇综述从信息、激励、风险与契约选择整理代理研究，区分实证代理理论与委托—代理研究两条路径，并提出可供组织研究检验的命题。

阅读抓手 · 需要概念地图时先读。跟踪行为型契约与结果型契约的区分，再追问行为可观察性与结果不确定性如何改变选择。

讨论问题（导读者提出） · 在什么条件下，增强结果导向的报酬会把过多风险转移给代理人？

摘要与导论，pp. 57–59；本地已有 PDF。 原文入口

核心必读 · 所有人重点阅读 · Aiden 报告

Jensen & Meckling (1976)
Theory of the Firm: Managerial Behavior, Agency Costs and Ownership Structure

Journal of Financial Economics 3: 305–360

核心问题 · 当决策被委托给利益未完全一致的行动者时，会发生什么？

作者的开篇论点 · 论文结合代理、产权与金融理论解释所有权结构，分析外部股权和债务带来的代理成本、这些成本由谁承担，以及存在代理成本的安排为何仍可持续。

阅读抓手 · 重建所有者兼经理的例子；分清代理成本的定义与投资者如何对预期经理行为定价的论证，并分别记录外部股权和债务的机制。

讨论问题（导读者提出） · 为什么缔约双方仍可能选择代理成本为正的所有权安排？

摘要与导论中的研究问题，pp. 305–308；本地已有 PDF。 原文入口

核心必读 · Chunyang 报告

Aghion & Tirole (1997)
Formal and Real Authority in Organizations

Journal of Political Economy 105(1): 1–29

核心问题 · 谁拥有正式决策权，谁实际决定最终选择？

作者的开篇论点 · 正式权威（formal authority）指决策的权利；实际权威（real authority）指对决策的有效控制。信息获取连接两者：授权可促进代理人的主动性，同时减少委托人的控制。

阅读抓手 · 跟踪双方获取信息的激励；理解保留正式否决权为何未必带来有效控制，并观察过载、决策紧迫性和偏好一致性在论证中的作用。

讨论问题（导读者提出） · 信息获取成本下降，会怎样同时改变主动性与控制？

摘要与导论，pp. 1–4；本地已有 PDF。 原文入口

打开完整双语深读 →

核心必读 · Yupeng 报告 · 最多 10 分钟

Holmström (1999)
The Firm as a Subeconomy

Journal of Law, Economics, & Organization 15(1): 74–102

核心问题 · 企业为何拥有资产？所有权怎样支持内部激励体系？

作者的开篇论点 · Holmström 以资产所有权连接企业边界与内部组织。控制资产带来缔约权，使企业能够在局部经济体系中安排任务、准入和激励。作者在导论中将这一解释定位为部分综合与理论猜想。

阅读抓手 · 沿 Alchian–Demsetz → 对 Hart–Moore 的批评 → 契约外部性与多任务激励阅读；重点解释，规制资产准入如何帮助设计合作。

讨论问题（导读者提出） · 限制个人的外部机会何时可能支持内部合作？又是什么约束这种权力？

摘要与导论，pp. 74–77；本地已有 PDF。 原文入口

打开完整双语深读 →

核心必读 · Yupeng 报告 · 最多 10 分钟

Gartenberg & Zenger (2023)
The Firm as a Subsociety: Purpose, Justice, and the Theory of the Firm

Organization Science 34(5): 1965–1980

核心问题 · 当员工成为企业成员时，权威需要维持哪些合作基础？

作者的开篇论点 · 作者在次级经济体视角上加入成员身份、共同目的与公正。员工以部分权利换取成员收益，并对权威如何行使控制形成期待；企业边界选择因此同时具有社会与经济后果。

阅读抓手 · 紧接 Holmström 阅读。跟踪加入组织时交换的权利、成员身份附带的期待，以及这些机制对权威和边界变化的含义。

讨论问题（导读者提出） · 什么可观察的结果能够区分“成员身份与公正”的解释和“激励体系”的解释？

摘要与开篇论证，p. 1965；本地已有 PDF。期刊卷期年份为 2023，在线发表年份为 2022。 原文入口

打开完整双语深读 →

核心必读 · 所有人重点阅读 · Chunyang 报告 · 最后读

Gibbons (2005)
Four Formal(izable) Theories of the Firm?

Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization 58: 200–245

核心问题 · 寻租、产权、激励体系与适应理论，解释的是同一个机制吗？

作者的开篇论点 · Gibbons 先用文字、再用形式模型比较四种理论的简化版本，最后将它们放入整合框架。他明确限定所讨论的是部分企业边界理论，并区分这些基础模型与原始理论的丰富内容。

阅读抓手 · 为每种理论分别记录：如何定义一体化、收益来自哪里、相应代价是什么；再用这张比较表回看 TCE 与 PRT。

讨论问题（导读者提出） · 实证研究需要测量什么，才能在这四种机制之间作出区分？

摘要与导论，pp. 200–203；本地已有 PDF。 原文入口
