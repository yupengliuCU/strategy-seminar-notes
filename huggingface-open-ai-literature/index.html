Hugging Face & the Open-AI Economy

A literature topic (non-course) in the shared Reading Notes site — the reading collection behind a research project on the Hugging Face platform, open-weight models, and the economics of open AI. English throughout. Source library: Dropbox/Hugging face/ (merged 2026-09-04): Part I = 01_Coauthor_Original_14 (14 core papers contributed by the group), Part II = 02_Project_Library_Added_21 (21 supporting classics pulled from projects, Zotero, and seminars).

How this topic is organized. Part I is the working core: platform-level studies of Hugging Face itself, the economics of open-weight models, and open-AI innovation and competition. Part II is the theoretical and empirical canon the core speaks to — open source and commons governance, platforms and ecosystems, and firm-level effects of open software and generative AI. Each paper deep dive is an AI-assisted reading note grounded in the full text (pdftotext of the local PDF), with quotes and numbers to check against the original; deep dives are being added progressively under Part I first. Pages use relative links (open via file:// or a local server). Canonical Obsidian notes are not mirrored for this topic (site only).
