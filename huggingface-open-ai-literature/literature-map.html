How to read the map. The two diagrams below answer different questions. Map A shows the direction of intellectual dependence: Part II theory streams feed the Part I empirical studies. Map B plots the 14 core papers by the question they answer, so you can see which pairs are in direct dialogue (e.g., Yue & Nagle vs. Azoulay et al. on whether openness pays; Wu et al.
